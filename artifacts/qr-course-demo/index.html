Evolutionary Psychology Course Demo

See how EvoPsych turns a four-week evolutionary psychology syllabus into 29 focused micro-lectures with examples, an AI tutor, adaptive practice, and AI-graded assignments.

Video outline and transcript

  1. Course dashboard: the demo opens with the course schedule, lecture progress, and assignment overview.
  2. Weekly foundations: Week 1 introduces natural selection, adaptation, and the first lecture, “What evolutionary psychology is and why it exists.”
  3. Ask the tutor: learners can ask a question about the exact passage they are reading and receive section-scoped guidance.
  4. Analytics: the course shows practice performance and conceptual strengths so learners can focus their next session.
  5. AI-graded work: homework and tests are graded for conceptual precision, with feedback that points to the next improvement.
  6. Learning loop: read the idea, ground it in a real example, state it precisely, and practice until it sticks.
